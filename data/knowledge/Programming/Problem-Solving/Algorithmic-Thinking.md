# Algorithmic Thinking Checklist

1. What is the **input**?  
2. What is the **output**?  
3. **Representation** — list, grid, graph, map, tree?  
4. **Brute force** — naive solution?  
5. **Constraints** — why is brute force too slow?  
6. **Pattern** — known technique (DP, greedy, graph, …)?  
7. **Optimize** — cache subproblems? shrink search?  
8. **Edge cases** — what breaks?  
9. **Debug** — reproduce, isolate, fix.  

Also: solve with math when the problem is quantitative; profile when the bottleneck is performance (see Systems-Performance).
