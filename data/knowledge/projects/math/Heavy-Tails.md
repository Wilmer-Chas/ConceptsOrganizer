# 4. Randomness → Why Averages Lie (Heavy Tails)

## Math idea

Not all randomness concentrates around the mean.

## Project

**Earthquake magnitudes or wealth model**

## Setup

- Generate events from:
  - normal distribution
  - power-law distribution

## Observe

- Mean exists but is meaningless
- Rare events dominate totals

## Math forced

- Variance divergence
- Expectation vs typical outcome

## Programming

- Histogram
- Cumulative contribution plot

## Key reveal

“The average is not the typical case.”
